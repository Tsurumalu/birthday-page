Place your images, photos, and audio files in this folder.

Recommended:
- birthday-photo.jpg       — a photo for the card or hero background
- song.mp3                  — background music (optional)
- memories/                 — a subfolder for timeline photos

Reference files in HTML like:
  <img src="assets/photo.jpg" alt="description" />

Or in CSS like:
  background-image: url('../assets/photo.jpg');
